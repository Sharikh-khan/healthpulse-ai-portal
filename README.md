# 🏥 HealthPulse AI Smart Health Portal

An AI-driven public health awareness and clinical guidance portal built with Streamlit and powered by Google Gemini. The platform provides interactive symptom guidance, disease prevention tips, a localized hospital directory, and personal health vitals tracking.

---

## ✨ Features

- **💬 Clinical AI Assistant:** Real-time, context-aware health guidance powered by Google Gemini (reading active patient profile and vitals).
- **🦟 Seasonal Disease Insights & FAQs:** Structured symptom, prevention, and emergency warning information for common seasonal illnesses.
- **🏥 Hospital Directory:** Localized healthcare facilities filterable by medical specialties and emergency/ICU availability.
- **📊 Vitals & Patient Profile:** Dynamic tracking of patient health metrics (Blood Pressure, Heart Rate, Allergies) integrated into the AI chat context.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend & UI:** [Streamlit](https://streamlit.io/)
- **Data Handling:** [Pandas](https://pandas.pydata.org/)
- **AI Model:** [Google Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/) (`google-genai` SDK)
- **Visualization:** [Plotly](https://plotly.com/python/)
- **Mapping & Reports:** [Folium](https://python-visualization.github.io/folium/) / [FPDF](https://pyfpdf.readthedocs.io/)
