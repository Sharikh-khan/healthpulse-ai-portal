Call 112 (national emergency number) or 108 (ambulance) right away for chest pain or pressure, trouble breathing, signs of stroke, severe bleeding that does not stop, seizures, loss of consciousness, severe allergic reaction with swelling of the face or throat, poisoning or overdose, and thoughts of self-harm. Stroke signs include a drooping face, weakness in one arm and slurred speech, and time matters. Do not wait for symptoms to improve and do not drive yourself if you feel faint. Stay with the person and keep them calm until help arrives.
