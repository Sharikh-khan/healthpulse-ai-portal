A fasting blood glucose below 100 mg/dL is typical. A fasting value of 100 to 125 mg/dL is in the prediabetes range, and 126 mg/dL or more on two separate tests suggests diabetes. HbA1c below 5.7 percent is typical, 5.7 to 6.4 percent is the prediabetes range and 6.5 percent or more suggests diabetes. Diabetes is diagnosed by a doctor using laboratory tests, not by a single home reading. Common symptoms include increased thirst, frequent urination, tiredness and blurred vision. A glucose below 70 mg/dL can cause shakiness, sweating, confusion and fainting and needs quick treatment with fast-acting sugar, and urgent help if the person cannot swallow or is unconscious.
