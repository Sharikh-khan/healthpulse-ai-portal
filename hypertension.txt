A resting blood pressure below 120/80 mmHg is generally considered typical for adults. Readings that stay at or above 140/90 mmHg on repeated measurement in a clinic are usually treated as high blood pressure, and a doctor confirms the diagnosis. High blood pressure often has no symptoms, so regular checks matter. Lifestyle steps that help include eating less salt, staying physically active, keeping a healthy weight, limiting alcohol and avoiding tobacco. Anyone with repeated high readings should see a doctor. A reading of 180/120 mmHg or higher together with chest pain, severe headache, breathlessness, weakness or confusion is an emergency and needs immediate care.
