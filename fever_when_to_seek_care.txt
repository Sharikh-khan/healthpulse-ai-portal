A temperature of 38 degrees Celsius (100.4 F) or higher is generally counted as fever. Most fevers from common viral infections settle with rest, fluids and light clothing. See a doctor if fever lasts more than three days, keeps rising, returns after improving, or comes with a rash, severe headache, pain on passing urine, or ongoing vomiting. Go to emergency care for fever with stiff neck, confusion, trouble breathing, seizures, fainting or signs of dehydration such as very little urine. Any fever in a baby under three months old needs urgent medical review. Fever during dengue or malaria season deserves an earlier check.
